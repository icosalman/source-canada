About

An independent, evidence-first publication on molecular sequence science.

BioResearch publishes clear, carefully sourced articles on molecular sequence science, for researchers, students and anyone who wants to understand what the literature actually says.

What we cover

  • Mechanisms: how synthesized sequences are synthesised, how they bind their targets and how they are broken down.
  • Study design: what separates a strong study from a weak one, and how to read results critically.
  • Evidence reviews: summaries of the published research on individual compounds, with citations.

Our standards

Every article cites primary sources. We say where evidence comes from cell or animal models rather than human trials, and we update articles when new research changes the picture.
